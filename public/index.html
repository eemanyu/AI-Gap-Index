04 QUICK ANSWERS

Anthropic vs OpenAI, explained.

What does the Anthropic vs OpenAI GAP Index measure?

It compares the relative attractiveness of Claude and ChatGPT subscriptions and ecosystems across plan value, model capability, coding and agent workflows, integrations, and distribution. Each monthly score is an editorial assessment linked to dated evidence—not a survey of all users.

Does a higher score mean Claude is always better than ChatGPT?

No. Above 50 means the editorial balance leans toward Anthropic; below 50 leans toward OpenAI; 50 means parity. A single score cannot capture every use case. Coding-heavy work, research, image generation, enterprise controls, privacy needs, and personal usage limits can lead to different choices.

Where can I compare current ChatGPT and Claude prices?

Prices, model access, and usage limits change. Always verify them on the official ChatGPT site and official Claude site before subscribing. This index does not promise real-time plan prices.

Can an AI agent query the index without scraping this page?

Yes. Use /api/index.json, /api/history.json, and /api/news.json. The site also publishes llms.txt and a Markdown overview for simpler tool ingestion. Observe the caveat and use source links before repeating a score as fact.
